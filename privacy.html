Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ahmed X (“we,” “us,” or “our”) may process information when you use the HAVENX Discord bot and related HAVENX services (“Service”). Use the Service only if you understand this policy.

1. Information that may be processed

Depending on which features are used and what the HAVENX client reports, the Service may process:

  • Discord information: Discord user ID, username/display name, server and channel identifiers, and command or interaction details needed to respond and administer the bot.
  • Roblox/HAVENX usage information: Roblox username and user ID, session status and timestamps, first/last seen information, session counts, and relevant game/place or server-session identifiers.
  • Technical and security information: executor name/version or a device identifier such as HWID only when the client reports it and the feature is enabled; these values may be unavailable or inaccurate.
  • Usage analytics: HAVENX client version, executor name/version, Roblox user ID/username, launch timestamps, place ID, and country/time-zone metadata where available.
  • Approximate country: the client may request public-IP geolocation from IPWho (ipwho.is). That provider receives the request IP and may process it under its own policies. HAVENX stores the returned country code/name and time-zone metadata, not the raw IP address. VPNs, proxies, network routing, and failed lookups can affect accuracy.
  • Content you submit: text or parameters sent through bot commands, notifications, announcements, support/administrative workflows, or HAVENX features.
  • Operational records: command outcomes, security events, and service diagnostics needed to operate and protect the Service.

2. Why information is used

Information may be used to operate bot commands, manage supported HAVENX sessions, show online/status information, respond to authorized administrative requests, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Service providers and disclosures

The Service uses Discord to deliver bot functionality and may use Firebase/Google Cloud to store and process operational records; it may also query public Roblox services to resolve Roblox usernames and IPWho for approximate country/time-zone lookup as described above. Information may be processed by these providers as needed to provide their services. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect the Service or users, or where you direct us to do so.

4. Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including operation, security, moderation, and historical usage features. Some records may remain after a session ends. Operational cleanup and retention periods may depend on the specific feature and configuration. We do not promise that every record is retained for a fixed period or can be deleted immediately from backups or provider systems.

5. Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect Service data, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not submit passwords, authentication tokens, payment data, or other secrets through bot commands.

6. Your choices and requests

You may stop using the Service at any time. To ask about information associated with your use or request correction/deletion where applicable, contact ax1_x on Discord and provide enough information to identify the relevant record without sending passwords or secrets. We may need to verify the request and may retain some data where required for security, legitimate operations, or legal compliance.

7. Children and eligibility

The Service is intended for people who are permitted to use Discord and the related platforms under their applicable rules. The operator does not knowingly seek to collect more information from children than is necessary for the Service.

8. International processing and policy changes

Service providers may process information in the regions where their infrastructure operates. We may update this policy when features or requirements change; the date above indicates the latest revision.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: contact the project owner on Discord using the username ax1_x.
